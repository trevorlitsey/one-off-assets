DO NOT MISS 01
trastevere

supplì

the molten rice croquette that starts the trip right

rome’s essential fried snack: tomato-seasoned risotto wrapped around mozzarella, breaded, and fried until the cheese stretches “al telefono.” it matters because it is the city’s most democratic street-food bite — quick, hot, cheap, and unmistakably roman.

what to orderclassic supplì al telefono; add cacio e pepe if they have it.
recommended placesupplì roma / i supplì
addressvia di san francesco a ripa 137, 00153 roma rm, italy

DO NOT MISS 02
prati / cipro

pizza al taglio

rome by the slice, cut with scissors and sold by weight

rectangular pizza baked in trays, snipped to size, weighed, and eaten standing up. bonci made the format famous for long-fermented dough and seasonal toppings, but the ritual is pure everyday rome.

what to orderone simple slice and one seasonal slice; ask for them warmed.
recommended placebonci pizzarium
addressvia della meloria 43, 00136 roma rm, italy
open in google maps
HIGH PRIORITY 03
trastevere

trapizzino

a triangular pizza pocket filled like a roman stew

a modern roman street-food icon: crisp-edged pizza bianca folded into a pocket and filled with classics like chicken cacciatore or meatballs. it is fast, filling, and ideal for two people sharing several flavors.

what to orderpollo alla cacciatora, polpetta al sugo, or lingua in salsa verde.
recommended placetrapizzino trilussa
addresspiazza trilussa 46, 00153 roma rm, italy

HIGH PRIORITY 04
campo de’ fiori / regola

pizza bianca con mortadella

warm bakery bread plus silky mortadella

rome’s simplest luxury: salty, oily pizza bianca split open and stuffed with paper-thin mortadella. roscioli’s forno is the classic stop because the bread is the point — fragrant, blistered, and still warm if timing is lucky.

what to orderpizza bianca con mortadella, cut for two.
recommended placeantico forno roscioli
addressvia dei chiavari 34, 00186 roma rm, italy
open in google maps
DO NOT MISS 05
portuense / near trastevere station

maritozzo con la panna

the roman cream bun, best treated as breakfast or late-night dessert

a soft, lightly sweet bun split and overfilled with whipped cream. in rome it is nostalgia, pastry case theater, and a two-person smile all at once — especially when eaten fresh and messy.

what to orderclassic maritozzo con panna; coffee if you are there in the morning.
recommended placeil maritozzaro
addressvia ettore rolli 50, 00146 roma rm, italy
open in google maps
WORTH A DETOUR 06
campo de’ fiori / largo dei librari

filetto di baccalà fritto

salt cod in a shattering fried jacket

roman-jewish and centro storico frying culture in one hand-held filet: salted cod, soaked, battered, and fried. dar filettaro keeps the dish wonderfully specific — you go for the baccalà, not a long menu.

what to orderfiletto di baccalà fritto; squeeze lemon and eat hot.
recommended placedar filettaro a santa barbara
addresslargo dei librari 88, 00186 roma rm, italy
open in google maps
WORTH A DETOUR 07
jewish ghetto / sant’angelo

carciofo alla giudia

the crisp fried artichoke of the jewish ghetto

a whole artichoke pressed open and fried until the leaves become crisp chips around a tender heart. it is one of rome’s most important dishes, rooted in the jewish quarter and best eaten in season when artichokes are at their peak.

what to ordercarciofo alla giudia; share one before heavier dishes.
recommended placenonna betta
addressvia del portico d’ottavia 16, 00186 roma rm, italy

four-food minimum

if time, appetite, or lines force hard cuts, do not leave rome without the core four: supplì first, bonci pizza al taglio second, trapizzino third, maritozzo when the cream-bun window opens.
